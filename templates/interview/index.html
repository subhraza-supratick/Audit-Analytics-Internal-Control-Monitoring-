Technical Audit Testing Standards & FAQs
1. What is the fundamental difference between an audit exception and fraud?
An audit exception is a red flag indicating a failure or departure from internal control policies (e.g., missing purchase order, after-hours posting, duplicate invoice). It is an evidentiary signal requiring human auditor investigation and substantive review, not definitive proof of illegal fraud or criminal intent.
2. How are duplicate invoices detected across disaggregated ledgers?
The system executes grouping queries across vendor ID, invoice date, and billing amount with HAVING COUNT(*) > 1 filters. For fuzzy matching, character normalization and window functions (RANK() OVER) catch subtle variations in invoice reference numbering.
3. Why is unsupervised outlier detection utilized alongside rule-based tests?
Deterministic rules only catch known historical evasion patterns. Unsupervised statistical algorithms (Isolation Forest and Z-score distributions) identify multi-dimensional structural anomalies in spend magnitude, timing, and velocity without requiring historical labeled fraud examples.
4. How is the explainable risk score (0-100) calculated?
To maintain transparency for regulatory bodies, the platform combines deterministic rule severity weights, transaction dollar magnitude, historical deviation factors, and statistical anomaly metrics into a standardized 0-100 composite score with explicit explanation breakdowns.
5. How does the ETL pipeline maintain data integrity and quality?
The ingestion engine checks file schema, enforces non-null constraints on primary transactional fields, verifies foreign-key counterparties, strips erroneous currency formatting, and computes an automated Data Quality Score prior to database persistence.
6. How is Segregation of Duties (SoD) enforced in the platform?
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) separates responsibilities across Administrator, Senior Auditor, Financial Analyst, and Compliance Viewer roles, ensuring that individuals who ingest data cannot unilaterally close or reclassify audit exceptions without review logs.
