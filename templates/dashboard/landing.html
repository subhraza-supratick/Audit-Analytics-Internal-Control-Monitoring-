Detection Logic

The 11 Internal Control Heuristics

Every transaction is tested against these deterministic rules and statistical models simultaneously.

01
Duplicate Invoice Detection
Flags invoices with identical vendor, amount, and date representing double-billing or accidental resubmission.
02
Duplicate Payment Detection
Identifies vendor payments processed more than once for the same invoice — a critical disbursement control failure.
03
3-Way Match Verification
Reconciles PO quantity and price against invoice amount and payment — flags deviations exceeding tolerance thresholds.
04
Split Purchase Structuring
Detects multiple POs to the same vendor just below executive authorization ceilings — a classic approval-bypass pattern.
05
Weekend & Holiday Disbursements
Flags payments or invoices raised on non-business days when authorisation controls are typically inactive.
06
Round-Sum Invoice Analysis
Identifies discretionary invoices with perfectly round amounts (e.g. ₹50,000.00), a common indicator of fictitious billing.
07
High-Risk Vendor Screening
Checks transactions against vendors flagged as high-risk, dormant, restricted, or with missing PAN/GST registration.
08
Benford’s Law Analysis
Applies first-digit frequency distribution to flag amounts deviating significantly from naturally occurring digit patterns.
09
Employee Expense Outliers
Flags claims statistically extreme relative to historical averages, or duplicate expense submissions by the same employee.
10
Sequential Invoice Anomalies
Detects consecutive invoice IDs from the same vendor in tight timeframes, suggesting fabricated invoice sequences.
11
Isolation Forest ML Detection
Applies unsupervised machine learning to identify statistically abnormal transactions without requiring historical fraud labels.
