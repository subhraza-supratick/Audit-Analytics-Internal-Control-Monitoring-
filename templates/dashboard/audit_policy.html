Enterprise Internal Control Charter
SOX 404 & PCAOB Compliant

The Audit Analytics & Continuous Internal Control Monitoring Platform operates under strict corporate governance guidelines. All transaction monitoring algorithms, SQL audit heuristics, and machine learning anomaly scores adhere to regulatory mandates including the Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002 (Sections 302 and 404) and COSO Internal Control Framework.

Data Confidentiality & Immutable Audit Trail

All financial transactions ingested into the platform undergo cryptographic hashing and timestamped logging. No auditor or system administrator may alter historical invoice amounts, payment receipts, or employee expense claims without generating a traceable entry in the persistent AuditLog ledger.

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Access privileges strictly segregated between Auditor, Analyst, Executive Viewer, and System Admin roles.
  • Automated Red-Flag Heuristics: 11 deterministic rule tests operating alongside Scikit-Learn Isolation Forest algorithms.
  • Auditor Working Papers: Exception review notes and status updates recorded with immutable timestamp and reviewer metadata.
